# 60-second portfolio film

## Direction

Keep the visual language operational: customer-call waveform, evidence brief, branching agent sessions, diffs, tests, browser proof, and the final ship decision. Avoid generic AI imagery and talking-head footage until the closing invitation.

## Voiceover and shots

**00:00–00:06 — The claim**

Visual: a single Cursor/Codex session expands into a clean control-room view.

> Most teams use AI to type faster. I use it to compress the entire product loop.

**00:06–00:16 — Customer signal**

Visual: Granola call notes; highlight one exact sentence, then resolve it into an `evidence.md` brief.

> It starts with a real customer signal. I turn the call, support thread and telemetry into a one-page evidence brief.

**00:16–00:32 — Parallel work**

Visual: scouts, two architecture routes, then two isolated worktrees. Use split-screen and visible role labels.

> Then specialized agents work in parallel: scouts map the system, architects disagree on the approach, and builders implement bounded options in isolated worktrees.

**00:32–00:46 — Independent proof**

Visual: a reviewer checks the diff against acceptance criteria; tests run; the browser completes the user flow.

> Nothing ships because an agent sounds confident. A separate reviewer checks the diff against the brief. QA runs the real flow. Tests, traces and screenshots become the proof.

**00:46–00:55 — Human decision**

Visual: branches converge at a single “human gate”; one route ships and a lesson is written back into a skill.

> I make the final product call, ship the smallest strong version, and turn what we learned into a reusable skill.

**00:55–01:00 — Invitation**

Visual: Punyaslok on screen for the first time, followed by the email address.

> Less busywork. Better decisions. Faster product learning. If that is how you want to build, let’s talk.

## Production notes

- Format: 16:9 master, with a 9:16 crop planned from the start.
- Pace: one proof point every 6–10 seconds; no shot under 1.5 seconds.
- Sound: restrained mechanical pulse, keyboard texture, and one clean rise at the human gate.
- Caption every line. Keep labels large enough for LinkedIn and X playback.
- Suggested bench from your saved tools: Video-shotcraft for shot planning, Hyperframes or Remotion for motion, and Cap/OpenScreen for clean product capture.
