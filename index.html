06 / Design principles

The system gets faster.
The bar stays human.

01

Thin harness.
Fat skills.

Keep orchestration boring. Put method, judgment, examples and failure modes into reusable skills.

02

Deterministic floor.
Probabilistic ceiling.

APIs, scripts and tests handle certainty. Models interpret, compare and decide where judgment matters.

03

Disagreement
before code.

Two independent approaches are cheap before implementation and expensive after it.

04

Proof over
performance.

A confident agent is not evidence. A passing flow, a trace and a clean diff are.

05

Context is
a budget.

Load the right brief, decisions and files—not the entire history of the company.

06

Customer signal
should compound.

Every useful call improves the product, its examples, its skills and the next conversation.

I do not delegate
